▲ Boxed warning

Addiction, abuse, and misuse of oxycodone can lead to overdose and death. Acetaminophen has been associated with cases of acute liver failure, at times resulting in liver transplant and death. Prolonged use during pregnancy can result in neonatal opioid withdrawal syndrome.

Source: FDA label · updated Jun 3, 2026

From the FDA label

Indications and usage

For the management of pain severe enough to require an opioid analgesic and for which alternative treatments are inadequate. Because of the risks of addiction, abuse, and misuse with opioids, reserve for patients in whom alternative treatment options have not been tolerated or are not expected to be tolerated, or have not provided adequate analgesia.

Dosage and administration

Initiate the dosing regimen for each patient individually, taking into account the patient's severity of pain, patient response, prior analgesic treatment experience, and risk factors for addiction, abuse, and misuse. Monitor patients closely for respiratory depression, especially within the first 24 to 72 hours of initiating therapy.

Contraindications

Significant respiratory depression. Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment. Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus. Hypersensitivity to oxycodone, acetaminophen, or any component of the product.

Source: FDA label · updated Jun 3, 2026 · normalized in the BFF
