What pharmacies pay for a drug.

Search a drug, then see its NADAC acquisition cost, its brand and generic versions with their own prices, and its FDA label. Every figure carries its unit and its date.

This is an acquisition cost, not a retail price. NADAC reports what pharmacies pay to buy a drug, not what you pay at the counter, and not an insurance price.
